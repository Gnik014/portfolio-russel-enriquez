Market-wide comments

Themes from a made-up comment set, not individual responses. Wait times at mid-sized brands came up often. Digital notifications were praised at Northwind and Wide World. Several comments asked for clearer fee explanations, and a smaller group wanted more self-serve tools for routine changes.

No respondent names or raw comment rows are shown. This box is a short theme summary only.

Per-page analysis

Q3 shows a tight cluster at the top of the table. Service scores above 4.0 line up with higher recommendation. Mid-table brands lag more on follow-through than on product range. This note is a worked example of analyst commentary and can be hidden when the page is exported.
